It's been a while since I've written here. Life has been busy, and maintaining a personal website often falls to the bottom of the priority list. But today, I'm making a change.

## The Old Site

My previous website was a bit of everything - a personal showcase, a project portfolio, and a blog. It was built with Jekyll (which I still love), but over time it became a bit unwieldy. The design was complex, and adding new content felt like a chore.

## The New Direction

I've decided to simplify. Instead of trying to be everything to everyone, I'm focusing on what matters most to me: writing. The new site is stripped down to its essentials - a clean, terminal-inspired design that puts the content first.

## The AI Assistant

What's different this time? I'm using Cursor, an AI-powered coding assistant, to help me maintain and evolve the site. It's fascinating to work with an AI that understands both code and context. It's like having a pair programmer who can:

- Help refactor old code
- Suggest modern best practices
- Write new features
- Even help write blog posts (like this one!)

## The Future

I'm not sure where this will go, but I'm excited to have a simpler platform for sharing thoughts and ideas. The combination of Jekyll's simplicity and AI assistance means I can focus on writing while the technical details are handled efficiently.

Here's to new beginnings and the power of human-AI collaboration!
